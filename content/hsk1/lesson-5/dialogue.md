# 第五课 会话 - Lesson 5 Dialogue
## 她女儿今年二十岁。 - Her Daughter Is Twenty Years Old This Year.

### 会话一 Dialogue 1

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 你家有几口人？ | Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén? | How many people are in your family? |
| B: 我家有三口人。 | Wǒ jiā yǒu sān kǒu rén. | There are three people in my family. |

### 会话二 Dialogue 2

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 你女儿几岁了？ | Nǐ nǚ'ér jǐ suì le? | How old is your daughter? |
| B: 她今年四岁了。 | Tā jīnnián sì suì le. | She's four years old this year. |

### 会话三 Dialogue 3

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 李老师多大了？ | Lǐ lǎoshī duō dà le? | How old is Teacher Li? |
| B: 她今年50岁了。 | Tā jīnnián wǔshí suì le. | She's 50 this year. |
| A: 她女儿呢？ | Tā nǚ'ér ne? | What about her daughter? |
| B: 她女儿今年20岁。 | Tā nǚ'ér jīnnián èrshí suì. | Her daughter is 20 this year. |
