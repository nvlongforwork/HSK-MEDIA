# 第三课 拼音 - Lesson 3 Pronunciation
## 你叫什么名字？ - What's Your Name?

### Initials: j, q, x vs. z, c, s

| Palatal (j, q, x) | Flat Tongue (z, c, s) |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| j — like "j" in "jeep" (tongue against hard palate) | z — like "ds" in "beds" (tongue against teeth) |
| q — like "ch" in "cheese" (aspirated, tongue against hard palate) | c — like "ts" in "cats" (aspirated, tongue against teeth) |
| x — like "sh" in "she" (tongue against hard palate) | s — like "s" in "see" (tongue against teeth) |

**Examples:**

| Palatal | Flat Tongue |
|---------|-------------|
| jī (chicken) | zī (capital) |
| qī (seven) | cī (porcelain) |
| xī (west) | sī (silk) |

### Vowels: i, u, ü

These three vowels have distinct mouth shapes:

| Vowel | Mouth Shape | Example |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| i | spread lips, tongue forward | jī, qī, xī |
| u | rounded lips, tongue back | zhū, chū, shū |
| ü | rounded lips, tongue forward | jǔ, qǔ, xǔ |

### Tone Change of 不 (bù)

不 changes its tone depending on the tone of the following syllable:

| Before Tone | Change | Example |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| 1st tone | bù (4th tone) | 不吃 bù chī |
| 2nd tone | bù (4th tone) | 不来 bù lái |
| 3rd tone | bù (4th tone) | 不好 bù hǎo |
| 4th tone | bú (2nd tone) | 不是 bú shì |

### Spelling Rules for ü

When ü appears after j, q, or x, the two dots are omitted:

| Actual Sound | Written As | Example |
|-------------|-----------|--------|
| jü | ju | 举 jǔ (lift) |
| qü | qu | 去 qù (go) |
| xü | xu | 需 xū (need) |

When ü appears after l or n, the two dots are kept:

| Written | Example |
|---------|--------|
| lü | 绿 lǜ (green) |
| nü | 女 nǚ (female) |
