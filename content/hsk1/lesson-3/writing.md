# 第三课 汉字 - Lesson 3 Writing
## 你叫什么名字？ - What's Your Name?

### Basic Strokes (3)

| Stroke | Name | Pinyin | Direction | Example |
|--------|------|--------|-----------|--------|
| ㇆ | horizontal-turning-hook | héngzhégōu | →↓↰ | 月 yuè, 买 mǎi |
| ㇃ | reclining-hook | wògōu | ⌒↗ | 心 xīn |

### Simple Characters (独体字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 月 | yuè | 4 | moon, month |
| 2 | 心 | xīn | 4 | heart, mind |
| 3 | 中 | zhōng | 4 | middle, center |
| 4 | 人 | rén | 2 | person, people |

### Stroke Order Rules (1)

When writing Chinese characters, follow these basic rules:

| Rule | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Horizontal before vertical | Write horizontal strokes before vertical strokes | 十 — first 一 (horizontal), then 丨 (vertical) |
| Left-falling before right-falling | Write left-falling strokes before right-falling strokes | 人 — first 丿 (left-falling), then ㇏ (right-falling) |
