# 第三课 语法 - Lesson 3 Grammar
## 你叫什么名字？ - What's Your Name?

### 课文 Dialogues

**Dialogue 1**
A: 你叫什么名字？ Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? — What's your name?
B: 我叫李月。你呢？ Wǒ jiào Lǐ Yuè. Nǐ ne? — My name is Li Yue. And you?
A: 我叫大卫。 Wǒ jiào Dàwèi. — My name is David.

**Dialogue 2**
A: 你是老师吗？ Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma? — Are you a teacher?
B: 不是，我是学生。 Bú shì, wǒ shì xuésheng. — No, I'm a student.

**Dialogue 3**
A: 你是中国人吗？ Nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma? — Are you Chinese?
B: 不是，我是美国人。 Bú shì, wǒ shì Měiguó rén. — No, I'm American.

### 语法要点 Grammar Notes

#### 1. Interrogative Pronoun 什么

什么 (shénme) means "what" and is placed where the answer would go in the sentence:

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| 你叫什么名字？ | 我叫李月。 |
| What's your name? | My name is Li Yue. |

#### 2. 是 Sentences

是 (shì) means "is/am/are" and connects a subject with a noun or noun phrase:

| Affirmative | Negative |
|-------------|----------|
| 我是老师。 I am a teacher. | 我不是老师。 I am not a teacher. |
| 他是学生。 He is a student. | 他不是学生。 He is not a student. |
| 她是中国人。 She is Chinese. | 她不是中国人。 She is not Chinese. |

#### 3. 吗 Questions

Add 吗 (ma) to the end of a statement to turn it into a yes/no question:

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| 你是老师。 You are a teacher. | 你是老师吗？ Are you a teacher? |
| 他是中国人。 He is Chinese. | 他是中国人吗？ Is he Chinese? |
