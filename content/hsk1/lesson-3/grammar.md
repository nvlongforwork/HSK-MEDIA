# 第三课 语法 - Lesson 3 Grammar
## 你叫什么名字 - What's Your Name

### 1. 叫 (jiào) - To Be Called
Used for names.

**Structure:** Subject + 叫 + Name

> 我叫小明。— My name is Xiaoming.
> 她叫什么名字？— What's her name?

### 2. 什么 (shénme) - What
Question word that replaces the answer in the sentence.

**Structure:** Subject + Verb + 什么？

> 这是什么？— What is this?
> 你叫什么？— What are you called?

### 3. 谁 (shéi) - Who
Used like 什么 but for people.

**Structure:** Subject + 是 + 谁？

> 他是谁？— Who is he?
> 谁是老师？— Who is the teacher?

### 4. 哪 (nǎ) - Which
Used with measure words or in 哪儿/哪里.

> 你是哪国人？— Which country are you from?
> 哪个是你的？— Which one is yours?
