### The Neutral Tone (轻声)

Some syllables in Chinese are pronounced in a light, short tone called the neutral tone. The neutral tone has no tone mark.

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning |
|------|--------|--------|
| 妈妈 | māma | mother |
| 爷爷 | yéye | paternal grandfather |
| 奶奶 | nǎinai | paternal grandmother |
| 爸爸 | bàba | father |

The second syllable in these words is pronounced lightly, without a full tone.

### Pinyin Spelling Rules (1)

**Tone Mark Placement**

The tone mark is placed on the main vowel of a syllable, following this order of priority:

1. If there is an **a** or **e**, the tone mark goes on it: dà, méi
2. If there is **ou**, the tone mark goes on the **o**: dōu, gǒu
3. Otherwise, the tone mark goes on the **last vowel**: guì, liú

**Abbreviations in Pinyin**

When certain finals combine with initials, they are abbreviated:

| Full Form | Written As | Example |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| iou | iu | liù (six) |
| uei | ui | guì (expensive) |
| uen | un | wèn (ask) |
