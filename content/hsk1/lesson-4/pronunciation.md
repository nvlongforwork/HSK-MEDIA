# 第四课 拼音 - Lesson 4 Pronunciation
## 她是我的汉语老师。 - She Is My Chinese Teacher.

### Initials: zh, ch, sh, r (Retroflex Consonants)

zh, ch, sh, r are retroflex consonants — the tongue tip curls back to touch the hard palate:

| Initial | Description | Similar To |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| zh | unaspirated, tongue curls back and touches hard palate | "j" in "judge" (no puff of air) |
| ch | aspirated, tongue curls back then releases with airflow | "ch" in "church" (strong puff) |
| sh | tongue curls back but does not touch, leaving a gap | "sh" in "she" (tongue curled) |
| r | like sh but with vocal cord vibration | "r" in "run" (tongue curled, voiced) |

|---|---|---|---|

### Front Nasal -n vs. Back Nasal -ng

| | n [n] | ng [ŋ] |
|---|-------|--------|
| Tongue position | Tip touches upper gum ridge | Back of tongue rises to soft palate |
| Mouth opening | Relatively small | Relatively wide |

**Practice Pairs:**

| Front Nasal (-n) | Back Nasal (-ng) |
|---|---|
| an | ang |
| ian | iang |
| uan | uang |
| en | eng |
| in | ing |
| uen | ueng |

### Tone Changes of 一 (yī)

一 changes its tone depending on the following syllable:

| Before Tone | Change | Example |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| 1st tone | yī → yì (4th tone) | 一张 yì zhāng (a sheet) |
| 2nd tone | yī → yì (4th tone) | 一条 yì tiáo (a strip) |
| 3rd tone | yī → yì (4th tone) | 一种 yì zhǒng (a kind) |
| 4th tone | yī → yí (2nd tone) | 一定 yídìng (certainly) |
| Alone or as a number | No change | 第一 dì yī (first), 一二三 yī èr sān |

### Pinyin Rules (3): Usage of y and w

When a final starting with i, u, or ü has no initial, y or w is added:

| Starting sound | Final → Written form |
|---|---|
| i alone | i, in, ing → yi, yin, ying |
| i + vowel | ia, ie, iao, ian, iang, iong → ya, ye, yao, yan, yang, yong |
| iu | iu → you |
| u alone | u → wu |
| u + vowel | ua, uo, uai, uan, uang, ueng → wa, wo, wai, wan, wang, weng |
| ui, un | ui, un → wei, wen |
| ü | ü, üe, üan, ün → yu, yue, yuan, yun |
