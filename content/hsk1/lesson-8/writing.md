# 第八课 汉字 - Lesson 8 Writing
## 我想喝茶。 - I Want to Drink Tea.

### Simple Characters (独体字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 少 | shǎo | 4 | few, little |
| 2 | 个 | gè | 3 | measure word (general) |

### Character Structure (3): Top-Bottom and Top-Center-Bottom

Compound characters also include top-bottom structure and top-center-bottom structure.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Top-bottom (上下结构) | Made of two parts stacked vertically | 是 shì (is), 爸 bà (father) |
| Top-center-bottom (上中下结构) | Made of three parts stacked vertically | 茶 chá (tea), 高 gāo (tall) |

### Radicals: 钅 and 口

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 钅 (metal radical) | Related to metal | 钟 zhōng (clock), 钱 qián (money) |
| 口 (mouth radical) | Related to mouth | 吃 chī (eat), 喝 hē (drink) |
