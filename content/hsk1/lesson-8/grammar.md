# 第八课 语法 - Lesson 8 Grammar
## 我想喝茶。 - I Want to Drink Tea.

### 1. Modal Verb 想

想 (xiǎng) is placed before a verb to express a wish, desire, or plan.

> （1）我想学汉语。
> （2）明天我想去学校看书。
> （3）我想买一个杯子。

| Subject | 想 | Verb Phrase |
|---------|---|----------|
| 我 | 想 | 学汉语。|
| 我 | 想 | 去学校看书。|
| 我 | 想 | 买一个杯子。|

### 2. Interrogative Pronoun 多少

多少 (duōshao) asks about an unknown quantity, often when the number may be large. It is not limited to numbers above ten. A measure word after 多少 may be omitted in some contexts. To ask about a price, use "……多少钱？".

> （1）你们学校有多少（个）学生？
> （2）你有多少（个）汉语老师？
> （3）这个杯子多少钱？— How much is this cup? The price could be below ten yuan.

### 3. Comparing 几 and 多少

Both ask "how many?" Use 几 (jǐ) when you expect a small number or ask for a number in a familiar set, such as a date or day of the week. 多少 (duōshao) is more open-ended and is also used for prices. These are common patterns, not a strict cut-off at ten.

| Question | Meaning | Why? |
|----------|---------|------|
| 你家有几口人？ | How many people are in your family? | A small number is expected. |
| 今天星期几？ | What day of the week is it? | Choosing from a familiar set. |
| 你们学校有多少学生？ | How many students are in your school? | An open-ended quantity. |
| 这个杯子多少钱？ | How much is this cup? | Asking for a price. |

### 4. Measure Words 个 and 口

个 (gè) is the most common measure word in Chinese. It is generally used before nouns that do not have a specific measure word.

> （1）三个老师
> （2）五个学生
> （3）一个杯子

口 (kǒu) is a measure word used to describe the number of family members (see Lesson 5).

> （1）李老师家有六口人。
> （2）你家有几口人？
> （3）我家有三口人。

### 5. Money Expressions

The basic unit of Chinese currency (RMB) is 元 (yuán). In spoken Chinese, 块 (kuài) is commonly used instead.

| Written | Spoken | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| 一元 | 一块 | 1 yuan |
| 五元 | 五块 | 5 yuan |
| 十元 | 十块 | 10 yuan |
| 五十元 | 五十块 | 50 yuan |
| 一百元 | 一百块 | 100 yuan |
