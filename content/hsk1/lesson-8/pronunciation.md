# 第八课 拼音 - Lesson 8 Pronunciation
## 我想喝茶。 - I Want to Drink Tea.

### Tone Combinations (3): 3rd Tone + Other Tones

When two syllables are combined, the tone of each syllable may sound slightly different from when said alone. Here we practice 3rd tone combined with all four tones:

| 3rd + 1st | 3rd + 2nd | 3rd + 3rd | 3rd + 4th |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| shǒujī (cell phone) | shǒuzhuó (bracelet) | shǒubiǎo (watch) | shǒutào (gloves) |

### Note on 3rd Tone Combinations

When the 3rd tone is followed by a 1st, 2nd, or 4th tone, it becomes a half-3rd tone (211) — the pitch dips down but does not rise back up. When followed by another 3rd tone, the first 3rd tone changes to a 2nd tone (the Third Tone Sandhi rule).
