# 第十三课 汉字 - Lesson 13 Writing
## 他在学做中国菜呢。 - He Is Learning to Cook Chinese Food.

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | 日 | rì | 4 | sun, day |
| 2 | 目 | mù | 5 | eye |
| 3 | 习 | xí | 3 | to practice |

### Radicals: 日 and 目

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 日 (sun radical) | Related to sun and time | 时 shí (time), 早 zǎo (early) |
| 目 (eye radical) | Related to eyes and seeing | 看 kàn (to look), 睡 shuì (to sleep) |
