# 第一课 语法 - Lesson 1 Grammar
## 你好 - Hello

### 1. 你好 (nǐ hǎo) - Basic Greeting
The most common Chinese greeting. Literally "you good."

**Structure:** 你 + 好

> 你好！— Hello!
> 您好！— Hello! (polite)
> 大家好！— Hello everyone!

### 2. 很 (hěn) - The Adjective Connector
In Chinese, adjectives need 很 before them in simple statements.

**Structure:** Subject + 很 + Adjective

> 我很好。— I'm fine. (I very good)
> 他很好。— He's fine.

### 3. 呢 (ne) - Return Question Particle
Used to ask "what about...?" after someone answers.

**Structure:** Subject + 呢？

> A: 你好吗？— How are you?
> B: 我很好，你呢？— I'm fine, and you?

### 4. 也 (yě) - Also
Placed before the verb/adjective.

**Structure:** Subject + 也 + Verb/Adjective

> 我也很好。— I'm also fine.
> 他也好。— He's also good.
