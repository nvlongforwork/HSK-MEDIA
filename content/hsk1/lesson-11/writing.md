# 第十一课 汉字 - Lesson 11 Writing
## 现在几点？ - What Time Is It?

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 午 | wǔ | 4 | noon |
| 2 | 电 | diàn | 5 | electricity |

### Radicals: 阝 and 亻

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 阝 (mound radical) | Related to terrain and locations | 院 yuàn (courtyard), 阳 yáng (sun) |
| 亻 (person radical) | Related to people | 你 nǐ (you), 他 tā (he) |
