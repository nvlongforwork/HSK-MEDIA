# 第十一课 语法 - Lesson 11 Grammar
## 现在几点 - What Time Is It

### 1. Telling Time 几点
**Structure:** Number + 点 (+ Number + 分)

| Time | Chinese |
|------|---------|
| 3:00 | 三点 |
| 3:10 | 三点十分 |
| 3:30 | 三点半 |
| 3:05 | 三点零五分 |

> 现在几点？— What time is it?
> 现在下午三点半。— It's 3:30 PM.

### 2. Time as Adverb
Time goes BEFORE the verb.

**Structure:** Subject + Time + Verb

> 我三点去学校。— I go to school at 3.
> 他上午来。— He comes in the morning.

### 3. 去/来/回 - Directional Verbs
- 去 (qù): go (away from speaker)
- 来 (lái): come (toward speaker)
- 回 (huí): return

> 我去学校。— I go to school.
> 他来中国。— He comes to China.
> 她回家。— She goes home.

### 4. 什么时候 (shénme shíhou) - When

> 你什么时候去？— When are you going?
> 他什么时候来？— When is he coming?
