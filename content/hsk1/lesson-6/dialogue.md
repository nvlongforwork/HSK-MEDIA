# 第六课 会话 - Lesson 6 Dialogue
## 我会说汉语。 - I Can Speak Chinese.

### 会话一 Dialogue 1

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 你会说汉语吗？ | Nǐ huì shuō Hànyǔ ma? | Can you speak Chinese? |
| B: 我会说汉语。 | Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ. | I can speak Chinese. |
| A: 你妈妈会说汉语吗？ | Nǐ māma huì shuō Hànyǔ ma? | Can your mom speak Chinese? |
| B: 她不会说。 | Tā bú huì shuō. | She can't. |

### 会话二 Dialogue 2

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 中国菜好吃吗？ | Zhōngguó cài hǎo chī ma? | Is Chinese food delicious? |
| B: 中国菜很好吃。 | Zhōngguó cài hěn hǎochī. | Chinese food is very delicious. |
| A: 你会做中国菜吗？ | Nǐ huì zuò Zhōngguó cài ma? | Can you cook Chinese food? |
| B: 我不会做。 | Wǒ bú huì zuò. | I can't cook. |

### 会话三 Dialogue 3

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 你会写汉字吗？ | Nǐ huì xiě Hànzì ma? | Can you write Chinese characters? |
| B: 我会写。 | Wǒ huì xiě. | I can write. |
| A: 这个字怎么写？ | Zhège zì zěnme xiě? | How do you write this character? |
| B: 对不起，这个字我会读，不会写。 | Duìbuqǐ, zhège zì wǒ huì dú, bú huì xiě. | Sorry, I can read this character but can't write it. |
