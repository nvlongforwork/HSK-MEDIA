# 第六课 语法 - Lesson 6 Grammar
## 我会说中文 - I Can Speak Chinese

### 1. 会 (huì) - Can/Able To (Learned Ability)
For skills acquired through learning.

**Structure:** Subject + 会 + Verb

> 我会说中文。— I can speak Chinese.
> 她会写中国字。— She can write Chinese characters.

**Negative:** Subject + 不会 + Verb

> 他不会读中文。— He can't read Chinese.

### 2. Verb + Object Structure
Many Chinese verbs are verb-object compounds.

> 说中文 — speak Chinese
> 读书 — read (books)
> 写字 — write (characters)

### 3. 在 (zài) + Place - Location
Express where something/someone is.

**Structure:** Subject + 在 + Place

> 我在学校。— I'm at school.
> 书在这儿。— The book is here.

### 4. Question with 会不会

> 你会不会说中文？— Can you speak Chinese?
> 他会不会写字？— Can he write characters?
