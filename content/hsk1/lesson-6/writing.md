# 第六课 汉字 - Lesson 6 Writing
## 我会说汉语。 - I Can Speak Chinese.

### Basic Strokes (6)

| Stroke | Name | Pinyin | Direction | Example |
|--------|------|--------|-----------|--------|
| ∠ | slanting-turning | piězhé | ↙→ | 么 me, 东 dōng |
| ㇂ | slanting-hook | xiégōu | ↘↗ | 我 wǒ, 钱 qián |
| ㇀ | upward-flick | tí | ↗ | 我 wǒ, 打 dǎ |

### Character Structure (1): Single-Component vs. Compound

Chinese characters have two basic structural types:

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Single-component (独体结构) | Made of only one part | 人 rén (person), 我 wǒ (I), 中 zhōng (middle) |
| Compound (合体结构) | Made of two or more parts | 你 nǐ (you), 做 zuò (to do) |
