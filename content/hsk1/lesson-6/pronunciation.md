# 第六课 拼音 - Lesson 6 Pronunciation
## 我会说汉语。 - I Can Speak Chinese.

### Tone Combinations (1): 1st Tone + Other Tones

When two syllables are combined, the tone of each syllable may sound slightly different from when said alone. Here we practice 1st tone combined with all four tones:

| 1st + 1st | 1st + 2nd | 1st + 3rd | 1st + 4th |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| kāfēi (coffee) | gōngyuán (park) | jīchǎng (airport) | chēzhàn (station) |

**Practice Words:**

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| jīntiān | jīnnián | jīngcǎi | chēpiào |
| gōngsī | gāngcái | cāochǎng | jīdàn |
| guā fēng | huānyíng | jīnglǐ | jīhuì |
| guānxīn | guānyú | kāishǐ | gāoxìng |

### Note on the 3rd Tone in Combinations

When the 3rd tone appears in a multi-syllable word, its pronunciation differs from the isolated 3rd tone (214). In combination, it becomes closer to a half-3rd tone (211) — the pitch dips down but does not rise back up.
