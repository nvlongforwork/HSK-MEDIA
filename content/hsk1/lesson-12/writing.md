# 第十二课 汉字 - Lesson 12 Writing
## 明天天气怎么样？ - How's the Weather Tomorrow?

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | 天 | tiān | 4 | sky, day |
| 2 | 气 | qì | 4 | air, gas |
| 3 | 雨 | yǔ | 8 | rain |

### Radicals: 女 and 饣

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 女 (woman radical) | Related to women and females | 姐 jiě (older sister), 妈 mā (mother) |
| 饣 (food radical) | Related to food and eating | 饭 fàn (rice/meal), 饮 yǐn (drink) |
