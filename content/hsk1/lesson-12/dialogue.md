# 第十二课 会话 - Lesson 12 Dialogue
## 明天天气怎么样？ - How's the Weather Tomorrow?

### 会话一 Dialogue 1

在路上 On the Road

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|----------|
| A: 昨天北京的天气怎么样？ | Zuótiān Běijīng de tiānqì zěnmeyàng? | How was the weather in Beijing yesterday? |
| B: 太热了。 | Tài rè le. | Too hot. |
| A: 明天呢？明天天气怎么样？ | Míngtiān ne? Míngtiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? | And tomorrow? How's tomorrow's weather? |
| B: 明天天气很好，不冷不热。 | Míngtiān tiānqì hěn hǎo, bù lěng bú rè. | Tomorrow's weather is great — not cold and not hot. |

### 会话二 Dialogue 2

在健身房 At the Gym

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|----------|
| A: 今天会下雨吗？ | Jīntiān huì xià yǔ ma? | Will it rain today? |
| B: 今天不会下雨。 | Jīntiān bú huì xià yǔ. | It won't rain today. |
| A: 王小姐今天会来吗？ | Wáng xiǎojiě jīntiān huì lái ma? | Will Miss Wang come today? |
| B: 不会来，天气太冷了。 | Bú huì lái, tiānqì tài lěng le. | She won't come — the weather is too cold. |

### 会话三 Dialogue 3

在病房 In the Hospital Room

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|----------|
| A: 你身体怎么样？ | Nǐ shēntǐ zěnmeyàng? | How is your health? |
| B: 我身体不太好。天气太热了，不爱吃饭。 | Wǒ shēntǐ bú tài hǎo. Tiānqì tài rè le, bú ài chī fàn. | I'm not feeling well. The weather is too hot, I don't feel like eating. |
| A: 你多吃些水果，多喝水。 | Nǐ duō chī xiē shuǐguǒ, duō hē shuǐ. | Eat more fruit and drink more water. |
| B: 谢谢你，医生。 | Xièxie nǐ, yīshēng. | Thank you, doctor. |
