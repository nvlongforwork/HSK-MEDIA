# 第十二课 语法 - Lesson 12 Grammar
## 明天天气怎么样 - How's the Weather Tomorrow

### 1. 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng) - How Is...?
Ask for opinion or description.

**Structure:** Subject + 怎么样？

> 天气怎么样？— How's the weather?
> 你怎么样？— How are you?
> 中国菜怎么样？— How's Chinese food?

### 2. 太...了 (tài...le) - Too...
Express excess.

**Structure:** 太 + Adjective + 了

> 太热了！— Too hot!
> 太好了！— Great! (Too good!)
> 太冷了！— Too cold!

### 3. 都 (dōu) - All/Both
Placed BEFORE the verb, after the subject.

**Structure:** Subject(s) + 都 + Verb

> 我们都是学生。— We are all students.
> 他们都很好。— They are all fine.
> 爸爸妈妈都在家。— Both mom and dad are at home.

### 4. 怎么 (zěnme) - How/Why

> 你怎么去学校？— How do you go to school?
> 这个字怎么读？— How do you read this character?
