# 第十五课 语法 - Lesson 15 Grammar
## 我是坐飞机来的 - I Came by Plane

### 1. 是...的 (shì...de) - Emphasizing Details
Emphasize how/when/where of a past action.

**Structure:** Subject + 是 + Detail + Verb + 的

> 我是坐飞机来的。— I came BY PLANE.
> 她是昨天来的。— She came YESTERDAY.
> 他是在北京工作的。— He works IN BEIJING.

### 2. Transportation 交通
Use 坐 (sit) for riding, 开 (drive) for operating.

| Chinese | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 坐飞机 | take a plane |
| 坐火车 | take a train |
| 坐出租车 | take a taxi |
| 开车 | drive a car |

> 你怎么去学校？— How do you get to school?
> 我坐出租车去。— I take a taxi.

### 3. 住 (zhù) - To Live
**Structure:** 住 + 在 + Place

> 我住在北京。— I live in Beijing.
> 你住在哪儿？— Where do you live?

### 4. 到 (dào) - To Arrive

> 我到了。— I've arrived.
> 你什么时候到北京？— When do you arrive in Beijing?
