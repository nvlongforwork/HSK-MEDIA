# 第十五课 语法 - Lesson 15 Grammar
## 我是坐飞机来的。 - I Came by Plane.

### 是……的 (shì...de) Construction

The 是……的 construction is used to emphasize the time, place, or manner of a past action. The action itself is already known; 是……的 highlights HOW, WHEN, or WHERE it happened.

**Structure:** Subject + 是 + Time/Place/Manner + Verb + 的

> 我**是**坐飞机来**的**。— I came **by plane**. (emphasis on manner)
> 他**是**昨天来**的**。— He came **yesterday**. (emphasis on time)
> 她**是**从北京来**的**。— She came **from Beijing**. (emphasis on place of origin)

The negative form uses 不是……的:

> 我不是坐火车来的。— I didn't come by train.
> 他不是昨天来的。— He didn't come yesterday.

Question form:

> 你是坐什么来的？— How did you come? (by what vehicle?)
> 你是从哪儿来的？— Where did you come from?
> 你是什么时候来的？— When did you come?

### Preposition 从 (cóng)

从 (cóng) means "from" and indicates a starting point.

**Structure:** 从 + Place + Verb

> 我从北京来。— I come from Beijing.
> 他从学校回来。— He comes back from school.
> 她从上海坐飞机来的。— She came from Shanghai by plane.

### Preposition 离 (lí)

离 (lí) expresses distance between two places.

**Structure:** A + 离 + B + 远/近

> 学校离这儿远吗？— Is the school far from here?
> 我家离学校很近。— My home is very close to the school.
> 北京离上海很远。— Beijing is very far from Shanghai.

### Transportation with 坐

坐 (zuò) is used with vehicles to express the means of transportation. 走路 (zǒu lù) means "to walk."

**Structure:** 坐 + Vehicle + Verb

> 坐飞机去。— Go by plane.
> 坐火车来。— Come by train.
> 坐出租车去。— Go by taxi.
> 走路去。— Go on foot.
