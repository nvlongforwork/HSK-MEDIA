# 第十五课 汉字 - Lesson 15 Writing
## 我是坐飞机来的。 - I Came by Plane.

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | 走 | zǒu | 7 | to walk |
| 2 | 坐 | zuò | 7 | to sit |
| 3 | 路 | lù | 13 | road |

### Radicals: 足 and 土

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 足 (foot radical) | Related to feet and walking | 路 lù (road), 跑 pǎo (to run) |
| 土 (earth radical) | Related to earth and ground | 坐 zuò (to sit), 地 dì (ground) |
