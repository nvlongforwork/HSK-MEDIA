# 第十五课 拼音 - Lesson 15 Pronunciation
## 我是坐飞机来的。 - I Came by Plane.

### Three-Syllable Tone Combinations (4): Starting with 4th Tone

When three syllables are combined, the tones interact in different ways. Here we practice combinations starting with the 4th tone:

| 4+1+1 | 4+1+2 | 4+1+3 | 4+1+4 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| dà jiāgōng (large processing) | bào xīnwén (report news) | diàn bīngguǒ (iced fruit) | zuò fēijī (take a plane) |

| 4+2+1 | 4+2+2 | 4+2+3 | 4+2+4 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| zuò chénggōng (achieve success) | diànhuájī (telephone set) | liànrénjiě (Valentine's Day) | dà lánqiú (play basketball) |

| 4+3+1 | 4+3+2 | 4+3+3 | 4+3+4 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| bàn dǎogōng (half-time work) | zuò huǒchē (take a train) | diànnǎobǎn (tablet) | dà kǒngquè (big peacock) |

| 4+4+1 | 4+4+2 | 4+4+3 | 4+4+4 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| dà dàngāo (big cake) | chūzūchē (taxi) | zuò diànnǎo (use computer) | dà shùlín (big forest) |
