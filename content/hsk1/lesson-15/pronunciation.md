# 第十五课 拼音 - Lesson 15 Pronunciation
## 我是坐飞机来的。 - I Came by Plane.

### Three-Syllable Tone Combinations: Starting with 4th Tone

The first fourth tone falls sharply. Practice these familiar HSK 1 phrases at a natural pace; the numbers show their written tones.

| Written tones | Example | Pinyin |
|---------------|---------|--------|
| 4 + 1 + 1 | 坐飞机 | zuò fēijī |
| 4 + 1 + 2 | 去中国 | qù Zhōngguó |
| 4 + 2 + 4 | 去学校 | qù xuéxiào |
| 4 + 3 + 1 | 坐火车 | zuò huǒchē |
| 4 + 4 + 3 | 看电影 | kàn diànyǐng |
| 4 + 4 + 4 | 看电视 | kàn diànshì |

### Hear the Destination or Activity

Compare 坐飞机 and 坐火车, then 看电影 and 看电视. The first word is the same within each pair; listen to the rest of the phrase before choosing an answer in the activity below.
