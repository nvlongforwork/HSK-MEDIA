# 第七课 拼音 - Lesson 7 Pronunciation
## 今天几号？ - What Is the Date Today?

### Tone Combinations (2): 2nd Tone + Other Tones

When two syllables are combined, the tone of each syllable may sound slightly different from when said alone. Here we practice 2nd tone combined with all four tones:

| 2nd + 1st | 2nd + 2nd | 2nd + 3rd | 2nd + 4th |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| shíjiān (time) | yínháng (bank) | cídiǎn (dictionary) | lánsè (blue) |
