### Character Structure (2): Left-Right and Left-Center-Right

Compound characters include left-right structure and left-center-right structure.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Left-right (左右结构) | Made of two parts side by side | 你 nǐ (you), 好 hǎo (good) |
| Left-center-right (左中右结构) | Made of three parts side by side | 谢 xiè (thank), 树 shù (tree) |

### Radicals: 氵 and 讠

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 氵 (three-dot water) | Related to water | 汉 hàn (Chinese), 没 méi (not have) |
| 讠 (speech radical) | Related to speech and language | 语 yǔ (language), 谁 shéi (who) |
