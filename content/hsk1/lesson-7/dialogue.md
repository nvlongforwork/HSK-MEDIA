# 第七课 会话 - Lesson 7 Dialogue
## 今天几号？ - What Is the Date Today?

### 会话一 Dialogue 1

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 请问，今天几号？ | Qǐngwèn, jīntiān jǐ hào? | Excuse me, what's today's date? |
| B: 今天9月1号。 | Jīntiān jiǔ yuè yī hào. | Today is September 1st. |
| A: 今天星期几？ | Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? | What day of the week is it? |
| B: 星期三。 | Xīngqī sān. | Wednesday. |

### 会话二 Dialogue 2

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 昨天是几月几号？ | Zuótiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | What was yesterday's date? |
| B: 昨天是8月31号，星期二。 | Zuótiān shì bā yuè sānshíyī hào, xīngqī èr. | Yesterday was August 31st, Tuesday. |
| A: 明天呢？ | Míngtiān ne? | And tomorrow? |
| B: 明天是9月2号，星期四。 | Míngtiān shì jiǔ yuè èr hào, xīngqī sì. | Tomorrow is September 2nd, Thursday. |

### 会话三 Dialogue 3

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|--------|
| A: 明天星期六，你去学校吗？ | Míngtiān xīngqī liù, nǐ qù xuéxiào ma? | Tomorrow is Saturday. Are you going to school? |
| B: 我去学校。 | Wǒ qù xuéxiào. | I'm going to school. |
| A: 你去学校做什么？ | Nǐ qù xuéxiào zuò shénme? | What are you going to school to do? |
| B: 我去学校看书。 | Wǒ qù xuéxiào kàn shū. | I'm going to school to read. |
