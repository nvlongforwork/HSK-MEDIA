# 第十四课 汉字 - Lesson 14 Writing
## 她买了不少衣服。 - She Bought Quite a Few Clothes.

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | 开 | kāi | 4 | to open, to drive |
| 2 | 车 | chē | 4 | car, vehicle |
| 3 | 回 | huí | 6 | to return |

### Radicals: 月 and 扌

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 月 (flesh/body radical) | Related to body parts | 服 fú (clothes), 脑 nǎo (brain) |
| 扌 (hand radical) | Related to hand actions | 打 dǎ (to hit/call), 找 zhǎo (to look for) |
