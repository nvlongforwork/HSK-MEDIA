# 第十四课 会话 - Lesson 14 Dialogue
## 她买了不少衣服。 - She Bought Quite a Few Clothes.

### 会话一 Dialogue 1

在宿舍 In the Dormitory

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|---------|
| A: 你去哪儿了？ | Nǐ qù nǎr le? | Where did you go? |
| B: 我去商店买东西了。 | Wǒ qù shāngdiàn mǎi dōngxi le. | I went to the shop to buy things. |
| A: 买了什么？ | Mǎi le shénme? | What did you buy? |
| B: 买了一点儿苹果。 | Mǎi le yìdiǎnr píngguǒ. | I bought some apples. |

### 会话二 Dialogue 2

在公司 At the Company

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|---------|
| A: 你看见张先生了吗？ | Nǐ kànjiàn Zhāng xiānsheng le ma? | Did you see Mr. Zhang? |
| B: 没看见。他不在。 | Méi kànjiàn. Tā bú zài. | No, I didn't. He's not here. |
| A: 他去哪儿了？ | Tā qù nǎr le? | Where did he go? |
| B: 他开车出去了，十分钟后回来。 | Tā kāi chē chūqu le, shí fēnzhōng hòu huílai. | He drove out. He'll be back in ten minutes. |

### 会话三 Dialogue 3

在商店门口 At the Shop Entrance

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---------|--------|---------|
| A: 你买了什么？ | Nǐ mǎi le shénme? | What did you buy? |
| B: 我买了不少衣服。 | Wǒ mǎi le bù shǎo yīfu. | I bought quite a few clothes. |
| A: 这些衣服都很漂亮啊！ | Zhèxiē yīfu dōu hěn piàoliang a! | These clothes are all so pretty! |
| B: 谢谢！ | Xièxie! | Thanks! |
