# 第十四课 语法 - Lesson 14 Grammar
## 她买了不少衣服。 - She Bought Quite a Few Clothes.

### 1. 了 (le) Indicating Completion or Occurrence

了 (le) placed after a verb indicates that an action has been completed or has occurred.

**Affirmative:** Subject + Verb + 了 + Object

> 她买了不少衣服。— She bought quite a few clothes.
> 我去商店买东西了。— I went to the shop to buy things.
> 他开车出去了。— He drove out.

**Negative:** Subject + 没(有) + Verb (no 了)

> 我没买衣服。— I didn't buy clothes.
> 他没看见张先生。— He didn't see Mr. Zhang.

Compare with 不: 我不买衣服 can describe a choice or habit ("I don't buy clothes"); 我没买衣服 means "I didn't buy clothes" on this occasion. For "do not have," use 没有 (Lesson 5).

**Question:** Verb + 了 + Object + 没有？

> 你买了东西没有？— Did you buy anything?
> 你看见他了没有？— Did you see him?

### 2. Noun 后 (hòu) — "After"

后 (hòu) is placed after a time expression to indicate "after" that period of time.

> 十分钟后回来。— Come back in ten minutes.
> 三天后。— After three days.
> 一个星期后。— After one week.

### 3. Modal Particle 啊 (a)

啊 (a) is placed at the end of a sentence to express strong emotion such as surprise, admiration, or excitement.

> 这些衣服都很漂亮啊！— These clothes are all so pretty!
> 太好了啊！— That's great!
> 你来了啊！— You're here!

### 4. Adverb 都 (dōu) — "All / Both"

都 (dōu) is placed before the predicate (a verb or an adjective) and refers back to all the items or people mentioned before it.

> 这些衣服都很漂亮。— These clothes are all very pretty.
> 我们都是学生。— We are all students.
> 他们都喜欢中国菜。— They all like Chinese food.

**Pattern:** Subject (plural or a group) + 都 + verb/adjective. In 我们都是学生, 都 comes before 是; in 这些衣服都很漂亮, it comes before 很漂亮.
