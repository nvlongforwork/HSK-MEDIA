# 第十四课 语法 - Lesson 14 Grammar
## 她买了很多衣服 - She Bought Many Clothes

### 1. Verb + 了 - Completed Action
了 after a verb shows the action is done.

**Structure:** Subject + Verb + 了 + (Quantity) + Object

> 她买了很多衣服。— She bought many clothes.
> 我吃了一个苹果。— I ate an apple.
> 他喝了三杯茶。— He drank three cups of tea.

### 2. 多少 (duōshao) - How Much/Many
For larger or unknown numbers (vs. 几 for small numbers).

> 多少钱？— How much money?
> 你有多少书？— How many books do you have?

### 3. 块 (kuài) - Yuan (Spoken Form)

> 这个多少钱？— How much is this?
> 十块钱。— Ten yuan.
> 太贵了！— Too expensive!

### 4. 一些 (yìxiē) - Some

> 买一些东西。— Buy some things.
> 我有一些书。— I have some books.
