# 第九课 汉字 - Lesson 9 Writing
## 你儿子在哪儿工作？ - Where Does Your Son Work?

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 在 | zài | 6 | at, in |
| 2 | 子 | zǐ | 3 | child, son |
| 3 | 工 | gōng | 3 | work, labor |

### Character Structure (4): Semi-Enclosed

Compound characters also include semi-enclosed structure, where the outer part partially wraps around the inner part. This includes two-sided enclosure and three-sided enclosure.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Semi-enclosed (半包围结构) | Outer part partially wraps around the inner part | 店 diàn (shop), 习 xí (to study), 这 zhè (this), 同 tóng (same), 医 yī (doctor) |

### Radicals: 辶 and 门

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 辶 (walking radical) | Related to walking or movement | 这 zhè (this), 送 sòng (to send) |
| 门 (door radical) | Related to rooms, doors, and entrances | 问 wèn (to ask), 间 jiān (room) |
