# 第九课 拼音 - Lesson 9 Pronunciation
## 你儿子在哪儿工作？ - Where Does Your Son Work?

### Tone Combinations (4): 4th Tone + Other Tones

When two syllables are combined, the tone of each syllable may sound slightly different from when said alone. Here we practice 4th tone combined with all four tones:

| 4th + 1st | 4th + 2nd | 4th + 3rd | 4th + 4th |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| miànbāo (bread) | miàntiáo (noodles) | diànnǎo (computer) | diànhuà (telephone) |
