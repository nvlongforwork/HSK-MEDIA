# 第十课 汉字 - Lesson 10 Writing
## 我能坐这儿吗？ - Can I Sit Here?

### Focus Characters (重点汉字)

| # | Character | Pinyin | Strokes | Meaning |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 上 | shàng | 3 | above, on |
| 2 | 下 | xià | 3 | below, under |
| 3 | 本 | běn | 5 | root; measure word for books |
| 4 | 末 | mò | 5 | end, tip |

### Character Structure (5): Fully-Enclosed

Compound characters include fully-enclosed structure, where the outer part completely surrounds the inner part on all four sides.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Fully-enclosed (全包围结构) | Outer part completely wraps around the inner part | 四 sì (four), 国 guó (country) |

### Radicals: 囗 and 礻

| Radical | Meaning | Examples |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 囗 (enclosure radical) | Generally indicates being enclosed or surrounded | 国 guó (country), 困 kùn (trapped) |
| 礻 (spirit radical) | Related to spirits, worship, fortune | 视 shì (to see), 祝 zhù (to wish) |
