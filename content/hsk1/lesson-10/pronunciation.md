# 第十课 拼音 - Lesson 10 Pronunciation
## 我能坐这儿吗？ - Can I Sit Here?

### Neutral Tone Syllables

The actual pitch of a neutral tone syllable depends on the tone of the preceding syllable. After 1st, 2nd, or 4th tone, the neutral tone drops lower. After 3rd tone, the neutral tone rises slightly higher.

| 1st + Neutral | 2nd + Neutral | 3rd + Neutral | 4th + Neutral |
|--------------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| zhuōzi (table) | pánzi (plate) | yǐzi (chair) | kùzi (pants) |

|---|---|---|---|
| bàba | māma | yéye | nǎinai |
| gēge | jiějie | dìdi | mèimei |
| gūgu | shūshu | tàitai | xīngxing |

### Suffixes: -们, -子, -头

Suffixes -们 (men), -子 (zi), and -头 (tou) are usually pronounced with a neutral tone.

**Practice Words - Suffix -们:**

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| nǐmen 你们 | wǒmen 我们 | tāmen 他们 | rénmen 人们 |

**Practice Words - Suffix -子:**

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| zhuōzi 桌子 | yǐzi 椅子 | bēizi 杯子 | bèizi 被子 |

**Practice Words - Suffix -头:**

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| shétou 舌头 | zhěntou 枕头 | shítou 石头 | mùtou 木头 |
| qiántou 前头 | hòutou 后头 | lǐtou 里头 | wàitou 外头 |
